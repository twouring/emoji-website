Why us

The ecosystem behind the Hub

Below is the environment and network we're part of, offered as background for this direction, not a promise of any specific outcome.

01

Taiwan's visa environment

Policies such as Taiwan's digital nomad visa give international talent an option for landing here. Specific eligibility and process depend on individual cases and current regulations; we offer no guarantee or on-your-behalf handling.

02

The TDNA network

Through the Taiwan Digital Nomad Association (TDNA), we connect to digital-nomad and startup organizations across Japan, Korea, Southeast Asia, and Europe. A possible connection point, not a guaranteed match.

03

Taiwan Digital Fest

An annual event gathering digital nomads, founders, and investors from home and abroad. An exchange occasion within the ecosystem, with no guaranteed participation or exposure outcome.

04

Talent density beside Taipei Main Station

The Hub sits at No. 11, Section 1, Chongqing South Road, right beside Taipei Main Station. The location favors everyday encounters, but does not represent any fixed matchmaking or support arrangement.

05

Government and enterprise ties

Years of accumulated government collaborations and enterprise connections form part of the ecosystem's background. Whether this extends to individual startup teams depends on future evaluation and each specific case.

06

The Hub's Stay Square event space

A shared coworking and event space at Stay Square, with high timber-beam ceilings and a large projection screen. It's the physical venue for the Hub's day-to-day operations, and the field where startup teams run real-world product validation, demos and events.
