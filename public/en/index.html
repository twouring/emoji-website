emoji — Taiwan Talent Hub

A hub of talent, right by Taipei Main Station.

A café, a late-night diner, a coworking space and a bookshop, each run on its own, gathered on one block. From eight in the morning until late at night, engineers, designers, founders and people living in Taiwan come and go.
