FAQ

What you need to know

What is emoji — Taiwan Talent Hub?

A multi-brand space by Taipei Main Station. 在咖啡 (at cafe) and 三點水 (3AM) welcome dining and client meetings without a membership; Rest Book offers reading and books for sale, personal study seats, showers and a lounge; Stay Square offers coworking, meeting rooms, phone booths, a tatami corner and event space.

Where is it?

No. 11, Section 1, Chongqing South Road, Zhongzheng District, Taipei, diagonally across from Exit Z10 of the Taipei Main Station underground mall.

What are the membership plans and prices?

Dining with at cafe or 3AM needs no membership. A 4-hour day pass is NT$200 and a 12-hour day pass is NT$500, with overtime at NT$100 per hour. Monthly membership is NT$4,000, quarterly is NT$10,000 and annual is NT$35,000; all include Stay Square access and free use of all facilities.

Is the hub open 24 hours?

The hub uses a split schedule, and 24-hour use of the Rest Book member space is conditional. Staffed hours are 08:00–17:00 and 17:00–03:00, when Dining with at cafe or 3AM and Stay Square coworking and event space are open. From 03:00–08:00 the hub is unstaffed, and members can access member-access areas by face recognition.

How do I apply for a community event or enquire about a private event?

Create your event in the emoji App (app.emoji.tw/host) and submit it for review; it is published after approval. Public events charge each guest a NT$150 venue and tea fee (one drink included). Buyout events are priced by space and group size: the Stay Square shared space from NT$5,000 (tiers of 10 guests, NT$500 per guest above 40), the Rest Book lounge NT$10,000 (up to 10 guests), and an at cafe / 3AM dining room buyout NT$30,000 (up to 50 guests), per 2-hour slot and fully creditable toward same-day food and drink. Tickets sold through emoji carry a 10% payment-collection fee.
