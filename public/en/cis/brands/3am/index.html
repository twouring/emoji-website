01

Naming

Write the name the same way everywhere. When pairing Chinese and English, use parentheses or a middle dot.

Chinese三點水

No quotation marks and no suffix such as “store”.

English3AM

Fixed spelling for running text. The “3am… Bistro” on the logo is lettering, not a way to set text.

Paired三點水 (3AM)

Titles and cards may also use “三點水 3AM”.

With the master brand3AM · emoji — Taiwan Talent Hub

A sub-brand never replaces the master brand. Documents, business cards, signage and the website show the master brand name or logo at least once. When the two logos sit side by side, separate them with a 1px rule or white space; never merge them into a new lockup.

02

Logo

A handwritten italic “3am…” wordmark with “Bistro”, a vertical “三點水” and a symbol of linked circles. The lowercase 3am is part of the lettering; in running text always write 3AM.

03

Social avatar

White on the brand color, 4500 px square. Fits the circular crop on Instagram, Facebook, LINE and Google Business Profile.

05

Usage

The sub-brand logos are the designer’s preliminary working version (2026-10-12). Until the final version is approved, these rules follow the master brand CIS.

Keep the proportions

Do not stretch, squash or slant it. No outlines, shadows or gradients.

Keep the colors

Only the brand color, white knockout and ink. Any other color scheme needs approval first.

Clear space

Leave at least ¼ of the wordmark height clear on every side.

A quiet background

Place it on paper, white or the brand color. On photos, only over clean, low-contrast areas.

No rearranging

Do not split the symbol from the wordmark, and do not retype the wordmark in a font. Ask the designer for new combinations.

Square files for avatars

Always use the square files in 04 for social avatars; do not drop the transparent logo onto a color block yourself.

Character moji · Nightfall

The late-night traveler. Drink lists, coasters. Eyes half closed, fewer words. In-store items at 3AM can use this moji pose. moji is always drawn in ink, never in the brand color.
