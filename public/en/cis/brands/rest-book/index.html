01

Naming

Write the name the same way everywhere. When pairing Chinese and English, use parentheses or a middle dot.

Chinese約書旅

No quotation marks and no suffix such as “store”.

EnglishRest Book

Fixed spelling for running text. The “Rest Book” on the logo is lettering, not a way to set text.

Paired約書旅 (Rest Book)

Titles and cards may also use “約書旅 Rest Book”.

With the master brandRest Book · emoji — Taiwan Talent Hub

A sub-brand never replaces the master brand. Documents, business cards, signage and the website show the master brand name or logo at least once. When the two logos sit side by side, separate them with a 1px rule or white space; never merge them into a new lockup.

02

Logo

A symbol of hill-like pages holding a “～” (open, it takes the background color), with the handwritten Rest Book wordmark and the “約書旅” Chinese wordmark with a few deliberate stroke breaks, stacked symbol → English → Chinese. Both wordmarks are outlined artwork; in running text always write Rest Book (capital R and B, one space).

Primary logo

03

Color

Each sub-brand has exactly one brand color, paired with the master brand’s paper and ink. The emoji yellow (#FFDE34) belongs to the master brand and is never used in sub-brand logos or brand color fields.

Mist Moss#687C70RGB 104 124 112Brand color: logo, avatar background, small color blocks
Paper#FBFAF6RGB 251 250 246Page background
Ink#1B1A17RGB 27 26 23Body text and headings

HEX / RGB are the screen reference; CMYK approx. 16 · 0 · 10 · 51 is a generic conversion, and no Pantone is specified. Mist Moss on paper is 4.27 : 1 — use it for the logo, graphics and large type, not small body text. Proof with the printer on the actual stock before printing.

05

Usage

Rest Book follows the Rest Book brand identity manual, CIS 1.0 (2026-11-04). This is a summary; see the source files for the full rules.

Keep the proportions

Do not stretch, squash or slant it. No shadows, outlines, gradients or 3D effects.

Three color versions

Mist Moss, ink, and paper-white knockout (on solid Mist Moss or ink). Never fill the open areas of the pages and “～”.

Clear space

At least x on every side, where x is half the height of “約”. The symbol alone needs ¼ of its height.

Minimum size

Vertical 240 px / 40 mm, horizontal 320 px / 55 mm, symbol + Chinese 160 px / 28 mm, symbol only 32 px / 8 mm. Smaller than that, switch to the symbol.

Never retype the wordmarks

The handwritten English and the broken-stroke Chinese are outlined artwork. Do not add strokes, close the breaks or retype them in a font.

No master-brand yellow

Never on a yellow background, never mixed with other brand colors, never across a busy photo.

Character moji · Rest

Able to nap even standing up. Seat cards, shower signs, quiet-area signs. In-store items at Rest Book can use this moji pose. moji is always drawn in ink, never in the brand color.
