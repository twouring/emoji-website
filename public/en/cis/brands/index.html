Master brand first

With the emoji logo, emoji goes on top or on the left; the sub-brand goes below or on the right.

Yellow is the master brand’s

#FFDE34 appears only in emoji layouts. Each sub-brand uses its own brand color.

Brands, not locations

Describe each place by its brand and function, never by its position in the building.
