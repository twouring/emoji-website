## 常見問題

- **需要會員嗎？** 不需要。三點水對所有訪客開放。
- **一個人可以來嗎？** 可以，吧檯很適合一個人。
- **只喝酒不吃東西可以嗎？** 可以，但有每人低消；以餐為主、酒為輔是我們的定位。
- **怎麼訂位？** 請用線上訂位；也歡迎直接來。

## FAQ (English)

- **Do I need a membership?** No. 3AM is open to everyone.
- **Can I come alone?** Yes, the counter is made for it.
- **Can I just have drinks?** Yes, within the per-person minimum spend; food first, drinks alongside is what we do.
- **How do I book?** Use the online reservation page; walk-ins are welcome too.
