## 常見問題

- **台北車站附近哪裡可以吃早午餐？** 在咖啡就在台北車站旁，重慶南路一段 11 號（站前地下街 Z10 出口斜對面），每日 08:00 開門。早午餐套餐 NT$200–400，都含一杯飲品。
- **早午餐幾點可以吃？** 早午餐全天供應：每日 08:00–17:00，16:30 最後點餐。
- **咖啡多少錢？有燕麥奶嗎？** 美式 NT$150、拿鐵 NT$180，其他義式咖啡 NT$180–190；拿鐵類可換燕麥奶 +30。套餐內含美式，換拿鐵 +30。
- **可以外帶嗎？** 可以。飲品與餐點都能外帶，店門口有外帶窗口；外帶不適用內用低消。
- **需要會員嗎？有低消嗎？** 不需要會員。平日內用每人一杯飲料，週六日內用每人低消 NT$300。
- **可以帶電腦工作嗎？** 可以。店內有插座與 Wi-Fi，白天的座位歡迎工作。
- **怎麼訂位？** 請用線上訂位；也歡迎直接來。

## FAQ (English)

- **Where can I have brunch near Taipei Main Station?** at cafe is right by Taipei Main Station at No. 11, Sec. 1, Chongqing S. Rd (diagonally across from Exit Z10 of the underground mall), open daily from 08:00. Brunch sets are NT$200–400 and each includes a drink.
- **What time is brunch served?** Brunch is served all day: 08:00–17:00 daily, last orders 16:30.
- **How much is the coffee? Is oat milk available?** Americano NT$150, latte NT$180, other espresso drinks NT$180–190. Milk drinks can switch to oat milk for +30. Sets include an Americano; a latte is +30.
- **Is takeaway available?** Yes. Drinks and food are available to go from the takeaway window; the dine-in minimum does not apply.
- **Do I need a membership? Is there a minimum spend?** No membership needed. Dine-in: one drink per person on weekdays; NT$300 minimum per person on Saturdays and Sundays.
- **Can I work here on my laptop?** Yes. There are power outlets and Wi-Fi, and the daytime seats welcome work.
- **How do I book?** Use the online reservation page; walk-ins are welcome too.
