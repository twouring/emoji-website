## 常見問題

- **台北車站附近哪裡可以吃早午餐？** 在咖啡就在台北車站旁，重慶南路一段 11 號（站前地下街 Z10 出口斜對面），每日 09:00 開門。早午餐套餐 NT$200–400，都含一杯飲品。
- **早午餐幾點可以吃？** 早午餐全天供應：每日 09:00–18:00，餐點最後點餐 17:00、飲品最後點餐 17:30。
- **可以外帶嗎？** 可以。飲品與餐點都能外帶，店門口有外帶窗口；外帶不適用內用低消。
- **內用有低消嗎？** 平日內用低消每人一杯飲料，週六日內用每人低消 NT$300。
- **內用有限時嗎？** 不限時。營業時間內（09:00–18:00）想坐多久都可以，平日低消每人一杯飲料。
- **可以帶電腦工作嗎？** 可以。店內有插座與 Wi-Fi，內用不限時，歡迎帶電腦工作。
- **怎麼訂位？** 請用線上訂位；也歡迎直接來。

## FAQ (English)

- **Where can I have brunch near Taipei Main Station?** at cafe is right by Taipei Main Station at No. 11, Sec. 1, Chongqing S. Rd (diagonally across from Exit Z10 of the underground mall), open daily from 09:00. Brunch sets are NT$200–400 and each includes a drink.
- **What time is brunch served?** Brunch is served all day: 09:00–18:00 daily; food last order 17:00, drinks 17:30.
- **Is takeaway available?** Yes. Drinks and food are available to go from the takeaway window; the dine-in minimum does not apply.
- **Is there a minimum spend for dine-in?** Dine-in: one drink per person on weekdays; NT$300 minimum per person on Saturdays and Sundays.
- **Is there a time limit?** No. Stay as long as you like during opening hours (09:00–18:00); one drink per person on weekdays.
- **Can I work here on my laptop?** Yes. There are power outlets and Wi-Fi, no time limit, and laptops are welcome.
- **How do I book?** Use the online reservation page; walk-ins are welcome too.
