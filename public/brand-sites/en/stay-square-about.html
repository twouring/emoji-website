About Stay Square

Stay a while. Work well.

Between transfers, meetings and the next stop: make waiting a beginning, not time to kill.

See the spacesSee plans

Our idea

A place to wait, and a place to work.

“等等” means “just a moment.” In the city, many people have two hours, half a day or an uncertain gap; there is no need to rush to the next place or keep ordering another drink.

We put together an environment for focus, a table for working things through, and room to run into new collaborators.

Keep your time for what you are doing.

By Taipei Main Station, where work, meetings and the next thing connect.
