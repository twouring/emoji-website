01Concept

A place to wait, and a place to work.

“等等” means “just a moment.” Between transfers, meetings, work and the last train, a little time does not have to be spent killing time. Open the laptop and move something forward.

Long tables, window seats, a tatami room, a four-person meeting room and phone booths make room for different ways of working through the same afternoon. Coffee, tea and snacks at the self-serve bar are included with a valid membership.

A day pass works for a gap in the day; monthly, quarterly and annual memberships are for those who work around Taipei Main Station often.

03How it works

Keep your time for what you are doing.

Choose a seat when you arrive. Use a phone booth when you need quiet; choose the long table or tatami room when you need to work together. The meeting room can be reserved.

    Questions

    What does membership include?

    A valid membership includes shared work areas, the meeting room, phone booth and self-serve bar, subject to on-site rules and reservations.

    Can non-members hire the venue?

    Yes. Sign in with an emoji account to book; no membership is needed. We confirm the booking after payment and refund in full if we cannot host it.

    Day passes follow the time purchased; regular memberships follow their validity. Actual opening times follow on-site notices.
