Shared work and meeting space · across from Taipei Main Station Exit Z10

Concept rendering24 hours

A place to wait, and a place to work.

“等等” means “just a moment.” Between transfers, meetings, work and the last train, a little time does not have to be spent killing time. Open the laptop and move something forward.

Long tables, window seats, a tatami room, a four-person meeting room and phone booths make room for different ways of working through the same afternoon. Coffee, tea and snacks at the self-serve bar are included with a valid membership.

A day pass works for a gap in the day; monthly, quarterly and annual memberships are for those who work around Taipei Main Station often.

What kind of seat do you need today?

All areas are included with a valid membership and available according to on-site conditions.

Questions

What does membership include? A valid membership includes shared work areas, the meeting room, phone booth and self-serve bar, subject to on-site rules and reservations.

Can non-members hire the venue? Yes. Submit an event or venue-hire application. Submission does not reserve the space; our team will confirm it.

How long can I use it? Day passes follow the time purchased; regular memberships follow their validity. Actual opening times follow on-site notices.
