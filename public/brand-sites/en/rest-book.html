Take me there

Your lounge by Taipei Main Station. Whether you’re waiting alone or have a few hours between connections, there’s a seat and a book here for you.

  1. A better way to wait

    Step out of the underground mall and leave the crowds behind. Read a chapter, answer a few emails, and head for your train when it’s time.

  2. No sign-up, no ticket

    Nothing to join. Pay the space-use fee for your time slot, drinks and showers included, and sit down — on your own between trains or with friends on a transfer.

  3. With a little extra

    Drinks, two showers, a big screen and board games in the lounge, all included with entry — and if you fall for a book, pay at the counter and take it on board.

04FAQ

Before you come

Is this a station lounge? Does my ticket get me in?

No. Rest Book is an independent reading space outside the station, not affiliated with TRA, HSR or the MRT. Tickets are not accepted; use is paid per the price list.

Can I buy the books?

Yes. Each book carries its own price; pay at the counter.

What kind of books are on the shelves?

Mostly work, starting up, travel and life in Taiwan, and the selection changes.

Can I shower? What should I bring?

Yes. The two showers are included in your entry, at no extra charge. Towels and toiletries can be bought on site; prices are posted there.

What’s the difference between entry and a quiet room?

Entry gives you the lounge, study seats, shelves, self-serve drinks and showers. A quiet room adds a one-person room of your own; its price already includes everything that comes with entry.

Same prices late at night?

Yes, one price list around the clock. After eight hours straight, please free the seat; you can re-enter and start again.

Can I stay overnight?

Rest Book is a reading space, not accommodation; quiet rooms are open and not lockable, and no bedding is provided.

How do I find you?

Diagonally across from Exit Z10 of the Taipei Main Station underground mall, No. 11, Sec. 1, Chongqing S. Rd.
