About at cafe

at cafe is a brunch and coffee spot by Taipei Main Station. Open sandwiches, croissants, bagels, onigiri and cold soba with an espresso-based coffee; by day it is also where people meet and work on laptops.

The room

at cafe sits in a 1969 house by Taipei Main Station. Bright and quiet by day, with a takeaway window: about 25 ping, 32 seats and a long counter, with outlets and Wi-Fi.

There is a wall of vinyl, and an elephant named moji hiding on another. A great many people pass Taipei Main Station every day; our goal is small: get one of them to sit down and finish the cup.

FAQ

Where can I have brunch near Taipei Main Station?

at cafe is right by Taipei Main Station at No. 11, Sec. 1, Chongqing S. Rd (diagonally across from Exit Z10 of the underground mall), open daily from 08:00. Brunch sets are NT$200–400 and each includes a drink.

What time is brunch served?

Brunch is served all day: 08:00–17:00 daily, last orders 16:30.

How much is the coffee? Is oat milk available?

Americano NT$150, latte NT$180, other espresso drinks NT$180–190. Milk drinks can switch to oat milk for +30. Sets include an Americano; a latte is +30.

Is takeaway available?

Yes. Drinks and food are available to go from the takeaway window; the dine-in minimum does not apply.

Do I need a membership? Is there a minimum spend?

No membership needed. Dine-in: one drink per person on weekdays; NT$300 minimum per person on Saturdays and Sundays.

Can I work here on my laptop?

Yes. There are power outlets and Wi-Fi, and the daytime seats welcome work.

How do I book?

Use the online reservation page; walk-ins are welcome too.
